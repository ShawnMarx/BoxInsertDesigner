# CCG example

A finished insert for a collectible card game box set: four decks in their own lift-out
holders, villain and rules cards, health dials, tokens and a folded board, in a 160 × 257 ×
62 mm box. It shows the whole workflow: components measured and photographed, layouts generated
and refined through change notes to an agent (each with its reply, in `spec.toml`), and a
tray edited by hand in SolidCraft (`edits/`).

The card, board and box art in `photos/` is pixelated (`tools/examples/pixelate.swift`), since it
is published art; component photos of plain pieces are as taken.

To open it, copy this folder into your games folder (`games/ccg-example/`) and pick it in the
app. Faces, builds and exports are generated from these files.
